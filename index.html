HOW IT WORKS

One portfolio. Two engines. One score.

The portfolio

$40,000 runs the futures engine: rules-based S&P 500 futures (ES / MES), with hard loss limits by the day, week and month.

$10,000 runs the options engine: Short-dated index options, sized up only after profits pay for it.

The campaign

Each calendar quarter starts fresh. The score is the whole portfolio’s return for the quarter and its deepest drawdown.

Returns are time-weighted from the daily account value, after commissions, with deposits and withdrawals taken out.

Your account

Alerts post in Discord as they happen. You place every trade yourself, in your own account.

Your money stays at your broker. We never have access.

READ THIS BEFORE YOU FOLLOW

The downside comes first.

  • 10% a quarter is an aggressive target. Expect quarters that miss it, including losing quarters.

  • Futures are leveraged. Losses can exceed the amount deposited.
  • The options sleeve can go to zero. Treat the full $10,000 as at risk.
  • Backtests and demo records are hypothetical. The live campaigns are the record that counts, and that record starts January 1, 2027.

Questions

What does “10% a quarter” mean?

It is the target for each calendar quarter: the whole portfolio’s return from the first trading day to the last, after commissions. It is a target, not a forecast. Some quarters will miss it.

How is drawdown measured?

From the highest account value reached during the quarter (the quarter’s starting value counts) to the lowest point after it, using daily closing values. The campaign aims to keep that under 10%.

Where do the live numbers come from?

From a live Interactive Brokers account, read every trading day through the broker’s own reporting. Only percentages are published. The account number and balance are not.

Why show backtests and demo records at all?

They are how the 10% target was set. They are hypothetical, so they sit in their own section, labeled, with the required statement. The live campaigns are the real record.

When does the first live campaign start?

January 1, 2027. It runs to the end of Q1 2027 and is scored the next trading day.

What happens in a losing quarter?

It is marked as a miss on the scoreboard and stays there. The next quarter starts a new campaign.

What do I trade?

S&P 500 futures (ES and MES contracts) and short-dated index options, from alerts posted in Discord. You place the trades in your own account.

Do I need $50,000?

Alerts are sized for a $50,000 portfolio. Book a call to talk through a different account size.
