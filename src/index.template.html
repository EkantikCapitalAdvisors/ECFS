How to read this board

Every quarter answers three questions, and only three:

  1. Did it reach {{target}}?
  2. Did the drawdown stay under {{ddLimit}}?
  3. What did the quarter teach?

A quarter is met only when the first two are both yes. Anything else is a miss, and it stays on the board.

Don’t add the quarters up. This is not a compounding strategy. Each quarter starts fresh, and some quarters will not be traded at all when conditions don’t fit the rules. Those are marked sat out, not met or missed. Adding quarterly returns together, or turning them into a yearly number, gives a figure this strategy does not aim for.

Questions

Is this a compounding strategy?

No. Each quarter is a separate campaign with its own target and drawdown limit. Gains are not stacked into a yearly figure, and some quarters will not be traded. Judge it quarter by quarter: target reached or not, drawdown held or not, and what was learned.

Why would a quarter not be traded?

When conditions don’t fit the rules, not trading is the plan. A quarter sat out is shown as “Sat out” on the scoreboard. It is not counted as met or missed.

What does “{{target}} a quarter” mean?

It is the target for each calendar quarter: the whole portfolio’s return from the first trading day to the last, after commissions. It is a target, not a forecast. Some quarters will miss it.

How is drawdown measured?

From the highest account value reached during the quarter (the quarter’s starting value counts) to the lowest point after it, using daily closing values. The campaign aims to keep that under {{ddLimit}}.

Where do the live numbers come from?

From a live {{live.broker}} account, read every trading day through the broker’s own reporting. Only percentages are published. The account number and balance are not.

Why show backtests and demo records at all?

They are how the {{target}} target was set. They are hypothetical, so they sit in their own section, labeled, with the required statement. The live campaigns are the real record.

When does the first live campaign start?

{{firstCampaignStart}}. It runs to the end of {{firstCampaignLabel}} and is scored the next trading day.

What happens in a losing quarter?

It is marked as a miss on the scoreboard and stays there. The next quarter starts a new campaign.

What do I trade?

S&P 500 futures (ES and MES contracts) and short-dated index options, from alerts posted in Discord. You place the trades in your own account.

Do I need {{minAccount}}?

Alerts are sized for a {{minAccount}} portfolio. Book a call to talk through a different account size.
