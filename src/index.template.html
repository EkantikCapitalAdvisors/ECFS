THE FLAW IN MOST PORTFOLIOS

Most portfolios ask one engine to do two jobs.

Cash flow and compounding pull in opposite directions. Withdraw from a compounding account and you cut the doubling short. Compound an income account and nothing reaches you this month.

So the work is split. One engine is built to be withdrawn from. The other is built never to be touched. Each keeps its own rules, its own record and its own stop.

CAUTION

Why 2x is the limit:

At 2x, each futures trade risks {{riskShare2}} of the futures account. That is the same share the published record was run at. Above 2x, you would be trading a size the record has never been tested at.

At 2x, the monthly loss limit is already {{monthlyPct2}} of the portfolio, and futures can gap past any limit.

If you would not sit through a full monthly loss limit at 2x, stay at 1x.

The 2x choice applies to the Cash-Flow Engine only. The Compounding Engine sizes itself: it grows only on earned profit, by its own ladder.

Questions

Why two engines instead of one?

Because cash flow and compounding pull against each other. Taking cash out of a compounding account cuts its doublings short. So one engine is built to pay you and the other is built to be left alone.

Should I run 1x or 2x?

We can’t tell you what suits you. Here is the difference: 2x doubles every loss limit and, with it, the monthly cash target. Same rules, twice the risk.

Can I go above 2x?

Please don’t. 2x is the size the published record was run at. Above it, the size is untested and every loss limit grows past what this page describes.

Why does nothing come out at first?

Three months of the target are banked first. That buffer is what keeps the withdrawal level through soft months.

What happens in a bad month?

The buffer pays the target. If the buffer falls below one month of target, withdrawals pause until it is back to three months.

What if an engine stops working?

Its stop rule fires, the stand-down posts in Discord within one trading day, and new trades stop. The other engine keeps its own rules.

Is the target realistic?

It is a target, not a record. It is set at {{r.target}}R a month, below the conservative planning rate of {{planning.annualR}}R a year. The record is young. Judge it trade by trade in Discord.

Can I take money from the Compounding Engine?

The design never does. Withdrawing from it cuts the doubling short.

Do I need {{minAccount}}?

Alerts are sized for {{minAccount}} at 1x. The rules are written in R, so they scale. Book a call to see them at your account size.
